Job Title Senior Software Engineer (Backend)
Department Engineering
Location Remote

Overview

We are looking for a skilled Senior Software Engineer to join our backend team. You will be responsible for building high-performance, scalable, and reliable services that power our core product. This role requires deep technical expertise in distributed systems and a passion for writing clean, maintainable code.

You will work closely with cross-functional teams to define architecture, influence product roadmap, and mentor junior developers.

Responsibilities

  • Design, develop, and deploy scalable backend services and APIs
  • Lead technical design and code reviews
  • Collaborate with product managers and frontend engineers
  • Troubleshoot and resolve production issues
  • Mentor junior engineers and support team growth

Requirements

  • 5+ years of experience in backend development (Python, Go, or Java)
  • Experience with cloud platforms (AWS/GCP) and microservices architecture
  • Strong understanding of database design (SQL and NoSQL)
  • Excellent problem-solving and communication skills
